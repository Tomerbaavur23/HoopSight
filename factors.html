Pickwise Factor Lab

Which of your own rules actually beats Sleeper's projection? For every finished game this season we compare what happened with what Sleeper projected, and check whether the opponent, the pace, a back-to-back or a missing teammate explains the gap.

Everything is computed in your browser from Sleeper's public data. With only a few weeks of games, treat anything without a check mark as unproven, and re-run every week as the sample grows.
