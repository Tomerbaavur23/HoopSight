Model settings

Projection source. Sleeper's own per-game projection is the base: in the backtest it beat both a random game and the Pickwise average-based model. Play the matchup starts from those projections and then adjusts picks for your matchup: it counts each game's chance that the player misses it, and when you are the favorite it prefers steadier games, when you are the underdog it accepts more risk for a higher ceiling. It also uses points already locked in this week. Max expected points simply takes the highest projection. The page shows your win chance under both, so you can see what the strategy changes. The miss-risk part is a model, not something proven on a large sample yet.

How it works. Your league, roster and matchup, the player stats, the schedule and the projections all come live from Sleeper's public data, using your league's own scoring. Nothing is stored anywhere except in this browser.

Not included. Rotation changes when a teammate is out, blowout risk, opponent defense and betting lines. Differences under about 1 point are noise. Check the official injury report before each tip-off.
