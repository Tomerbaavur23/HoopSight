Model settings

Pickwise projection: this season's average fantasy points at your league's scoring, adjusted for home or away and back-to-backs. Sleeper projection: Sleeper's own per-game projection. When a later game is within the margin of the top projection, the recommendation holds the later game, because injury and rest news keeps arriving until it starts. The miss-rate option moves picks earlier for players who often miss games, and it was tested on only 25 player-weeks.

How it works. Your league, roster and matchup, the player stats, the schedule and the projections all come live from Sleeper's public data, using your league's own scoring. Nothing is stored anywhere except in this browser.
