Scoring and model settings

Scoring starts from your Sleeper settings. Change any value and every projection updates.

Off by default. This season, 6 of the 8 games your players missed were the last game of their week. Turning this on lowers the projection of later games for players who miss games often, so the pick moves earlier. Tested on only 25 player-weeks, so use it as a second opinion.

When a later game is within this margin of the top projection, the recommendation holds the later game. Waiting brings more injury and rest news before you are locked in.

How a projection is built. Season-to-date per-game stat line, turned into fantasy points with your scoring, then adjusted for opponent scoring defense, game pace, home court, and back-to-backs (a bigger cut for players aged 35 and over).

Not in this version. Rotation changes from injuries, blowout risk, player-by-player variance, and betting lines. Only the five teams that allow the most and the fewest points are adjusted; all other opponents count as average. Double-double and bonus frequencies are my estimates from recent games. Technical and flagrant fouls are ignored.
